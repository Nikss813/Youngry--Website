What makes it different

Built so you never get bored

Youngry isn't a static menu — it's built to feel like you're actually asking someone "what should I eat today?"

🎲

Never the same meal twice in a row

Not feeling the first pick? Hit "I'm feeling hungry!" and Youngry rerolls that meal — it always skips whatever you just saw, so you're not stuck seeing the same dish twice.

🍳

Specific meals for specific times

The AI knows morning food from midnight food — breakfast, lunch, and dinner all pull from their own pool of dishes.

🕒

One at a time, on purpose

Choose exactly the meal you're deciding on instead of getting your whole day dumped on you at once.

🌍

Filipino to French, and everything between

Every pick comes from a mix of cuisines — Filipino, Japanese, Italian, Korean, Mexican, Indian, and more — so lunch never feels like it's on repeat.
